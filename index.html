FAQs

Straight Answers, No Guesswork

Answers to the questions Ridgetown homeowners ask us most.

Are your technicians certified and insured?

Certified and insured on every job, no exceptions. Ontario does not offer a trade licence for duct cleaning, so the credentials that count are training, certification, and insurance coverage, and we carry all three.

How often should ducts be cleaned in a town like Ridgetown?

With the tree canopy and the harvest traffic on Highway 3, every two to three years keeps most homes here ahead of it. Retired households running filters year-round sometimes stretch to four, but the first return grille inspection usually settles the question.

Should we time the cleaning around harvest?

Ideally, yes. Combines and wagons on Highway 3 trail field dust straight through town each October, and the returns collect their share of it. A cleaning in late October or November, after the harvest but before the furnace locks in for winter, means you are not heating through a season of what the trucks dragged in.

What happens during a cleaning and how long should I plan for?

Vacuum connected at the trunk, registers washed, every run brushed under suction, blower compartment cleaned, photos before and after. A typical Ridgetown home runs three to four hours; century houses with additions run longer.

Why do some rooms in our older home never get comfortable?

Frequently it is duct leakage or crushed runs robbing the far registers, common in houses whose basements became workshops. A pressure test locates the losses, and sealing returns the balance.

Do you work outside Ridgetown itself?

Yes, across the neighbouring Chatham-Kent communities including Blenheim, Highgate, Thamesville, Merlin, Bothwell, and into Chatham.

There is a smell when the furnace starts in fall. Can that be fixed?

Yes, and permanently. The residue causing it comes out with the cleaning, and a sanitizer applied to the clean duct walls handles what remains. The smell is memory after that, not maintenance.

We keep the windows shut and run filters. Why is the house still dusty?

Because the filter only treats air the system pulls through it, and a household that runs the fan steadily circulates whatever the returns hold. Closed-window living concentrates the load instead of diluting it. A clean system plus a better filter grade is the combination that finally settles it.

Do you serve areas outside of Ridgetown, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Ridgetown and nearby communities including Blenheim, Highgate, Thamesville, Merlin, and the surrounding area.
